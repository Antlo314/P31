# P31 build log

A running record of what has been built for Proverbs 31 Marketplace and The P31 Collective, written for the CEO's report. Newest first. Dates are 2026.

## October 11 — Team dashboards, shop controls, lesson videos, and walkthroughs of everything

- **Every team member gets her own dashboards.** The October 2026 team directory is built into both sites. Each mailbox on it is its own login: the @p31market.com ones belong to the marketplace side, the @thep31collective.org ones to the Collective, and a person with two mailboxes has two logins. In Systems → Team & dashboards, Melanie JC and the admins switch on exactly what each login opens: Content creator (Content Studio), Social media manager (posts, DMs and comments, growth), Emails (the shared inboxes and campaigns), Classroom (the mentorship consoles), People & applications, Vendors & forms, Markets & events, Orders, Curators & shops, and DaVinci. Database v41.
- **Curators & shops, inside Systems.** Every shop with its status, owner, products and orders. Approve, pause or decline a shop, feature it at the top of the directory, give badges, leave the vendor a note she sees in her studio, change its name or link, and hide or feature products one by one. "Message" opens a private conversation with the vendor in The Gathering.

Lessons can carry an uploaded video file (streamed inside the lesson) as well as a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link; sessions can carry an uploaded recording as well as a link. The same editors live in each mentor console.
- **Animated walkthroughs of every function.** Systems → Walkthroughs lists a chapter for every room on both sites and every other dashboard (Today, Content Studio, the email inbox, both mentor consoles, a student's classroom, The Gathering, the vendor's Curator Studio). Press Play and a pointer glides to each item and taps it while the card explains it, with a progress bar that advances by itself (hovering pauses it); or go step by step. A link can start any chapter, so a walkthrough can be sent to someone.
- **Test runs.** The same page holds 19 test runs: what to do, what should happen, how to clean up, with a checklist kept on the device and a printable sheet (also in `docs/TEST-WALKTHROUGHS.md`). Two automated checks run on the computer: the screen test (190+ screens on both sites) and a new link test that follows every internal link and image on both sites.

## October 11 — Faster pages, and a site that runs itself a little more

- **Lighter and quicker.** The 3D engine (Three.js, about half a megabyte) is gone from the site entirely now that no page uses it. Phones get a hero film a third the size (0.5 MB instead of 1.4 MB). The browser opens its connection to the database while the page is still loading, and fonts and icons are cached for a long time instead of re-checked. Pointing at a link (or touching it on a phone) starts loading that page before the click, so the next page appears almost at once.
- **No form email can get lost.** Until now the team only heard about a form if the visitor’s browser stayed open long enough to trigger the email. A scheduler now sweeps every 10 minutes, so every agreement, registration, survey, volunteer and application email goes out.
- **A morning email at 8 AM.** One summary of the last day across both sites: orders, applications, agreements, registrations, survey answers, volunteers, emails waiting, site errors, the posts going out today, and how many days until the next event, with one button into Today.
- **Approving a vendor places her in a market.** In Vendor applications, choosing a market while approving adds her to that lineup and names the market in her welcome email, which now walks her through confirm, register, create her account and sign the storefront agreement.
- **Already-approved vendors skip the wait.** When someone the team has already approved signs the Storefront Agreement, her copy includes the link to create her account right away instead of “within 7–10 days”, and the agreement is marked as sent.
- **Survey answers become invitations.** When a vendor market turns on “Date announced”, every business that said “I’m in for the next market” in the last six months is added to its lineup as invited and emailed a registration link. Markets now say whether they take vendors (the Luncheon does not) and when vendors check in.
- **My markets, in every vendor’s studio.** Each vendor sees her own markets: registered, confirmed, paid or waitlisted, her booth, the date and venue, and the check-in time, so “am I confirmed?” no longer needs an email.
- **Next month drafts itself.** On the 20th, Content Studio drafts next month’s engagement posts from the team’s last Auto-create settings, skipping days already booked, and emails marketing@ and founder@ to review them. Nothing posts until someone approves it. Two buttons on the Pre-posts tab do the same on demand (the rest of this month, or next month).
- **One admin list.** Admins were written into the code; they now live in the database and are added or removed in Systems → Team access (the site always keeps at least one).
- **Behind it.** Database v38 (site_admins, market vendors and check-in, survey invitations, my_markets, the digest log, and three scheduled jobs), two new functions (`team-digest`, `studio-autocreate`), and an updated `join-notify`. The Auto-create planner is shared between the Studio and the server, with a check that fails the build if the two copies drift. 143 screens in the automated test.

## October 11 — Auto-create in Content Studio

- **Auto-create.** A new room in Content Studio: choose the kinds of posts and their share (Bible fill-in-the-blank, finish the sentence, this or that, pop quiz, Sunday word, invitations, business tips), the look (five card styles: Night, Lavender, Ivory, Gold, Royal), the start date, how many days (7 to 90) and how often (daily to twice a week), and the times (from Zernio’s own engagement data, P31 defaults, or typed in per weekday). It skips days that already have a post. One tap drafts every card and caption.
- **Pre-posts.** The preview shows each card drawn in the browser with its caption and time; any can be swapped for another from the library, rewritten with AI, edited, retimed or removed. Then “Save as drafts” puts them in the Planner for review, or “Schedule all” draws, uploads and books the whole run to Instagram and Facebook with a progress bar. A Pre-posts tab lists everything generated and not yet sent, with approve and schedule-all-approved.
- **The library.** A starter set of 42 KJV verses with their blanked word, 14 Sunday verses, 12 sentence starters, 10 this-or-that choices, 7 quizzes, 5 invitations and 10 business tips, each used once before any repeats. The team adds its own in the Library tab and can switch any item off. Captions open with rotating hooks so a month of the same format never reads the same twice.
- **Behind it.** Cards are drawn in the browser with html-to-image (open source, MIT) using the site’s own fonts, so what you preview is what posts. Database v36 adds the library, the series record and the card on each planned post.
