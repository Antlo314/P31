# P31 build log

A running record of what has been built for Proverbs 31 Marketplace and The P31 Collective, written for the CEO's report. Newest first. Dates are 2026.

## October 14 (late) — Content on autopilot, with Melanie JC approving

- **The calendar fills itself.** Every morning the autopilot looks at what is booked. When the next stretch has fewer posts a day than wanted (two by default) and it starts within a week, it drafts the next two weeks: bold quotes in Melanie JC's card style, prayers, King James scripture from the verified list (on Sundays where it fits), fill-in-the-blank questions, and curator and mentorship invitations, at most one invitation a day. Each post gets its own card look, Instagram and Facebook captions (links on Facebook, "link in bio" on Instagram) and a time when followers are online, clear of the posts already booked. The lines and captions are written by the same AI the Studio uses, in her voice, never repeating the October collection or recent batches; if the AI is unavailable, a built-in library of lines takes over so a batch is never missed.
- **Nothing posts without a yes.** The approvers (ceo@p31market.com to start) get an email: "22 posts are ready for your yes", with the first lines and one button. The review page (made for a phone) shows every card as it will look, its time and caption; she can remove any post or change a caption, then tap Approve, and each card is drawn and scheduled on Instagram and Facebook. "Not this batch" sets it aside with a note. A reminder goes out after a day, the watchdog flags a batch still waiting, and a link expires after a week.
- **In the Studio.** Content Studio → Auto-create → Autopilot: switch it on or off, posts a day, days per batch, how far ahead to start, the mix of kinds, who approves, the latest batches with a Review button, and "Draft a batch now". Database v46 and a new `content-autopilot` function.

## October 14 (night) — The watchdog, and two clear doors for curators

- **A watchdog that checks everything, every hour.** It confirms Instagram and Facebook are connected in Zernio and warns two weeks before a login expires; retries any post that failed (once, by itself) and alerts if it keeps failing; warns when the next week has days with no post; re-sends form emails that did not go out and alerts if they still do not; flags applications and requests that have waited more than two days; and watches for bursts of site errors, scheduled jobs that fail or stop, database updates waiting, and email or Zernio not being set up. Each problem is one alert with a plain "how to fix it", in a new Watchdog panel at the top of Systems → Health and on Today. A new failure is emailed within the hour; anything still open is summarized at 8 AM. Alerts close themselves once the check passes; they can be snoozed or marked fixed. Database v45 and a new `watchdog` function.
- **Two separate pages for curators**, as Melanie asked. p31market.com/apply is now "Vend at a P31 Market" (a booth at our seasonal markets), and the new p31market.com/storefront is "Open your virtual storefront": what you get, the terms (three months complimentary, then $14.99 a month plus the 5% transaction fee), and the Storefront Agreement form that replaced the JotForm of the same name. Both open with the same two doors, so anyone who arrives on the wrong one can switch, and the footer, the phone menu, the vendor forms page, the shop directory and the About page each point to the right one. The old /vendors/agreement address still works.

## October 13 (evening) — Motion that feels finished, reels made the way Instagram wants them
