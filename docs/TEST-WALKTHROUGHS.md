## 1. Vendor application

*As a visitor · p31market.com · about 4 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open p31market.com/apply in a private window.
2. Fill in every field with TEST in the business name (for example “TEST Candle Co.”) and a real email you can read.
3. Tap the agreement box and submit.

**What should happen**

- A thank-you screen appears at once.
- Within a minute the market team mailbox (vendor@p31market.com) gets “New vendor application: TEST Candle Co.”.
- In Systems → Vendor applications the application is listed as New; Today and the morning email count it.

**Afterwards:** Decline it in Systems → Vendor applications (choose not to send the email), or leave it as a sample.

## 2. Storefront agreement

*As a visitor · p31market.com · about 4 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open p31market.com/vendors/agreement.
2. Fill in the business and contact (TEST in the name), tick “I agree”, sign with your mouse or finger, and submit.

**What should happen**

- A confirmation screen; the signer gets her copy by email with the terms.
- The team gets “Storefront agreement signed: TEST…”.
- Systems → Vendor forms → Agreements lists it, with “Pre-approve & email the account link”.

**Afterwards:** Leave it, or remove the row from Systems → Vendor forms.

## 3. Market registration into a lineup

*As an admin · p31market.com · about 5 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open p31market.com/vendors/register, pick a market, fill in TEST details, upload any small logo, sign and submit.
2. In Systems → Vendor forms → Registrations open the TEST registration and tap “Add to lineup”.
3. Open Systems → Markets & events → that market → Lineup.

**What should happen**

- The registrant gets “We received your registration”; the team gets the registration email.
- The lineup now lists TEST with her logo; the market page on p31market.com/calendar shows her in the lineup when it is public.

**Afterwards:** Remove TEST from the lineup in Markets & events.

## 4. Feedback survey and the next-market invite

*As an admin · p31market.com · about 5 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open p31market.com/vendors/feedback, rate the market, choose “I’m in for the next market!”, enter an email, submit.
2. In Systems → Markets & events create a market with Has vendors on and Date public on (a TEST date).

**What should happen**

- Systems → Vendor forms → Feedback shows the survey.
- The new market’s lineup contains the surveyed business as Invited, and she gets the invitation email with the registration link.

**Afterwards:** Delete the TEST market (its lineup goes with it).

## 9. Assign a dashboard and sign in as that person

*As an admin · both sites · about 6 minutes*

**Do this**

1. In Systems → Team & dashboards, on a team member’s login switch on one dashboard only (for example Orders).
2. Tap “Sign-up steps” and send them to her, or follow them yourself with that mailbox: create the account at thep31collective.org/portal, confirm the email, sign in.
3. Open thep31collective.org/systems as her.

**What should happen**

- She lands on My dashboards with that one dashboard; the menu shows only Orders and Walkthroughs; typing /systems/vendors in the address bar sends her back to My dashboards.
- Today shows only the cards for her dashboard. The Gathering lists her with her role badge (Executive Assistant, Marketing Strategist…).
- Switching the dashboard off in Team & dashboards removes it the next time she opens Systems.

**Afterwards:** Set her dashboards back to the ones she should have.

## 10. Pause, feature and note a shop

*As an admin · p31market.com · about 4 minutes*

**Do this**

1. In Systems → Curators & shops open a shop and set its status to Paused.
2. Open p31market.com/directory and the shop’s own link in a private window.
3. Set it back to Approved, switch Featured on, add a badge (Handmade) and a note, save.
4. Hide one product with the eye button, then show it again.

**What should happen**

- While paused the shop is missing from the directory and its link shows the shop as unavailable; the vendor still opens her studio and sees your note.
- Featured puts it at the top of the directory; the badge shows on the shop; the product disappears and returns on the storefront.

**Afterwards:** Leave the shop Approved and remove any test badge or note.

## 11. Lesson with an uploaded video

**Afterwards:** Delete the TEST module (its lessons and the video go with it).

## 13. An order from a shop to Systems

*As a vendor · p31market.com · about 6 minutes*

2. Open Systems → Orders.
3. Confirm the order, then mark it fulfilled.

**What should happen**

- Each status change shows in both places; the buyer gets the status emails.

**Afterwards:** Cancel the test order.

## 14. A reply from the shared inbox

*As a team member · thep31collective.org · about 3 minutes*

**What should happen**

- Your personal address receives the reply from members@.

**Afterwards:** None.

## 15. Schedule a post

## 17. The Gathering: post, thread, direct message, alert

*As a team member · both sites · about 5 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open The Gathering, post in The Gathering channel, react to a message, reply in its thread, attach a photo.
2. Open Members, pick someone, Message, send her a direct message, then close the tab without her reading it.

**What should happen**

- Everything appears for other members without a refresh.
- If she does not read the DM within a few minutes she gets one email with the message and a link back; with The Gathering open she gets a device notification instead.

**Afterwards:** Delete the test messages (the ⋯ menu on your own message).

## 18. Walkthroughs play on every dashboard

*As a team member · both sites · about 3 minutes*

**Do this**

1. In Systems → Walkthroughs press Play on “Systems at a glance”; let it run, then open one from Other dashboards (Content Studio).

**What should happen**

- The pointer glides to each item and taps it, the card explains it, and the progress bar advances by itself; hovering the card pauses it.
- Content Studio opens with its own walkthrough running.

**Afterwards:** None.

## 19. Automated screen, link and form check

*As an admin · both sites · about 2 minutes*

**Do this**

1. On the computer with the project: `npm run dev` in one terminal, then `node scripts/screen-test.mjs` and `node scripts/link-test.mjs` in another.

**What should happen**

- screen-test reports every screen opened with no crashes or missing buttons; link-test reports no broken internal links or images across both sites.

**Afterwards:** None (the tests use sample data, never the live database).
