## 1. Vendor application

*As a visitor · p31market.com · about 4 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open p31market.com/apply in a private window.
2. Fill in every field with TEST in the business name (for example “TEST Candle Co.”) and a real email you can read.
3. Tap the agreement box and submit.

**What should happen**

- A thank-you screen appears at once.
- Within a minute the market team mailbox (vendor@p31market.com) gets “New vendor application: TEST Candle Co.”.
- In Systems → Vendor applications the application is listed as New; Today and the morning email count it.

**Afterwards:** Decline it in Systems → Vendor applications (choose not to send the email), or leave it as a sample.

## 2. Storefront agreement

## 3. Market registration into a lineup

## 17. Edit and delete in every Systems room

*As an admin · both sites · about 6 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Submit a TEST vendor application and a TEST Join application (use your own email).
2. In Systems → Vendor applications open the TEST one → Edit details, change the phone, Save. Then Delete.
3. Do the same in Join applications, then in Vendor forms (an agreement, a registration, a volunteer), People (a TEST contact), and Campaigns → Subscribers (add, unsubscribe, delete a TEST address).

## 19. Design a post, and a reel with music

*As a team member · both sites · about 6 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open Content Studio → Design. The Collection shows the 48 quote cards on the calendar; open one.
2. Click the big words on the page and change them; drag them a little; open Styles and pick another colour style.
3. Open Motion and try Editorial, Gentle and Word by word (each plays as you pick it). Put one word in *asterisks*, pick Marker in its settings, and play again.
4. Open Music, play two tracks, pick one, then press Play at the top to watch and hear the reel.
5. Download → Reel 9:16 with music, and Download → Image (PNG). Then Save.
6. Use in a post → As a reel with music. On Create post, set a time a week out and schedule it, then delete it from the Calendar.

**What should happen**

- The words, colours and position change on the page as you edit; Undo puts them back.
- The MP4 is 9:16: the topo lines draw themselves in, the words arrive the way the Motion choice says, the highlighted word gets its marker after it lands, the camera eases in, and the music sits underneath; the PNG matches the page.
- Saved lists the design (for the whole team once v44 has run; in this browser before that).
- Create post opens with the reel attached, its cover (the finished card) and the caption filled in; the scheduled post shows on the Calendar with that cover.
- Trending Instagram tracks list under Pick a trending track once the Instagram account is connected to Zernio through Facebook Login; before that the Studio says so.

**Afterwards:** Delete the TEST post from the Calendar and the design from Saved.

## 20. The watchdog catches a problem and clears it

*As an admin · both sites · about 3 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open Systems → Health. The Watchdog panel at the top says when it last checked; press Run checks now.
2. If something is listed, read its Fix line and open it. Try Snooze a day on one alert.
3. Submit a TEST vendor application and, after a few minutes, run the checks again.

**What should happen**

- The panel answers “Everything is running” or lists each problem with how to fix it, and Today shows the same in its System check card.
- A snoozed alert is greyed out and no longer emailed; it still clears itself once the check passes.
- A brand-new failure (for example a post that keeps failing) is emailed to the alert list within the hour; anything still open is summarized at 8 AM.

**Afterwards:** Delete the TEST application.

## 23. The Gathering: @mention and search

*As a team member · both sites · about 4 minutes*

**Do this**

1. Open The Gathering on two devices (or two browsers) signed in as two team members. On the second, switch on “Notify me on this device” from her own card.
2. On the first, in The Gathering channel type @ and the first letters of her name, pick her from the list, finish the message and send.
3. On the second, stay in another room. Then open the magnifying glass, search a word from the message, and tap “Mentions of me”.

**What should happen**

- Her name is gold in the message for her (purple for everyone else) and the message has a gold edge.
- The room shows an @ marker in her channel list and her device shows “… mentioned you”.
- Search lists the message with the word marked; tapping it jumps to it (or opens its thread).

**Afterwards:** Delete the test message.
