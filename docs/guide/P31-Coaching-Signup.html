Steps 1 to 3

The intro call, then one button

Every client starts with a conversation. The agreements go out after it, never before.

1 · She asks for an intro call

On thep31collective.org/mentorship/business she either picks a time on the calendar or chooses to leave her details. Details she leaves arrive by email to the team and as a card under Mentor console → Intro calls. Calendar bookings come through Calendly as before.

2 and 3 · After you meet, send the three agreements

  • The site emails her the link straight away. You can also Copy link to text it, or Email it from my mailbox too.
  • Did she come through the calendar or by word of mouth? Use Mentor console → Agreements → Send the agreements and type her email. It is the same window.

Step 4 · What she sees

Three forms, signed in order

Made for a phone. The words are exactly those of the Google Forms, so nothing she agrees to has changed.

Step 5 · The mentor console

Follow her progress, then send her payment link

Mentor console → Agreements lists everyone who was sent the forms and what each one has signed.

  • View beside a signed form shows her answers and signature. The full PDF is already in the team inbox.
  • Send payment link appears once all three are signed and is set to the package she chose. It creates her private checkout, which works once and for 14 days. She creates her account with the same email, enters her card on Stripe, and her classroom opens. Her plan then renews monthly.
  • Need a different package after all? Use Invites → New invite and pick any plan.
  • Renew for 30 days brings back an expired link, Revoke link stops one, and Delete removes her and her signed forms.

One time

Setup, and the fallback

A few steps switch this on. After that it runs on its own.

  1. Run the database update (v47) from the project folder:
    npx supabase db query --linked --project-ref xsnhxjttdizljaawpumz -f storefront_v47_coaching_forms.sql
    It answers with one row: intakes_ready true, forms_ready true, sign_ready true, details_ready true, package_plans 4.
  2. Deploy the email functions, which send the links and the PDF copies:
    npx supabase functions deploy join-notify watchdog --no-verify-jwt --use-api --project-ref xsnhxjttdizljaawpumz
  3. Put the site live: tell Claude “merge it”.
  4. For payment links, connect Stripe: the Stripe secret key and the mentorship webhook, both in Supabase. Until then, everything above works except step 5.

If something goes wrong

She saysDo this
“I never got the email.”Ask her to check spam and promotions, or open Agreements → Copy her forms link and text it to her.
“My link has expired.”Agreements → her card → Renew for 30 days. The same link works again.
“I picked the wrong package.”Send her payment link from Invites → New invite with the right plan, and note it on her intro-call card.
“It will not let me sign the next form.”The forms go in order, so she needs to finish the one shown first. If the page itself fails, she can use the Google Form link at its foot.
