The P31 Collective by NEBA · Enrollment workflow

Start a new enrollment

For Faith Mentorship and Business Coaching. You press one button; the site walks her through the rest.

Your part: 3 clicks, right after the intro call

  1. Sign in at thep31collective.org/portal and open the Mentor console.
  2. Go to Intro calls, find her card and press Send the three agreements.
    She booked another way (calendar, phone, DM)? Use Agreements → Send the agreements and type her name and email.
  3. Check her name and email, leave the link at 7 days, and press Create and email the link. That is it.

She is asked to finish within 48 hours. The link keeps working for 7 days in case life happens.
