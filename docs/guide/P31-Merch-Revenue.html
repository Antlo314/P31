How to read this

  • Printful charges us is Printful's price for the blank item with our print, before shipping. Where colours cost different amounts, the sheet uses the dearest one, so real profit is the same or a little higher.
  • Stripe fee is the standard card rate, 2.9% + 30¢ per payment. Klarna and Afterpay cost more (about 6% + 30¢), and one payment covers the whole bag, so a bag of several pieces pays the 30¢ once.
  • Shipping: shoppers pay $6.95 per order, free over $75.00. Printful's shipping above is its standard rate for one piece to Georgia; extra pieces in the same box add only a little.
  • Not included: sales tax (Printful may add it in some states unless a resale certificate is on file with them) and any returns or reprints.
