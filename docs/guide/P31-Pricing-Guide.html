Administrator Guide: How to Change Pricing

Comprehensive operating guide for updating product prices, managing sale/compare-at rates, adjusting storefront subscriptions, and creating new products in the dashboard.

Prepared For: Admin & Management Team, P31 Marketplace
Dashboard Feature Version: v54 Live

1. Overview of Pricing Controls

As a P31 administrator, you now have complete control over all pricing across the platform directly from your Systems Dashboard (/systems/shops) and Curator Studio. Pricing changes update instantly in the Supabase database and reflect immediately on live shop pages.

1. Marketplace Products & Artisans

Adjust the selling price of any product across any vendor's storefront, set promotional compare-at ("Was") prices to trigger sale tags, or create brand-new items directly.

2. Storefront Plans & Billing Terms

Configure monthly vendor storefront subscription rates (standard $14.99/mo), free period durations (3 months), grace periods, and auto-pause rules in one central panel.

2. Method 1: The 1-Click Quick Price Editor (Fastest)

The fastest way to change a price without opening forms or modal windows is using the new Quick Price Editor.

HOW THE QUICK PRICE PILL LOOKS IN YOUR DASHBOARD:

Viewing Mode: $28.00 ✎
➔
Editing Mode (after clicking): $ [ 32.50 ] ✓ ✕
  1. Open Systems: Navigate to /systems/shops (Curators & shops).
  2. Open the Catalog: Click either the All Products & Pricing tab at the top, or select any specific shop from the left list.
  3. Click the Gold Price Pill: Click directly on the price (e.g. $28.00). It transforms into an active input field.
  4. Type New Price: Enter the new numeric price (e.g. 32.50).
  5. Save: Press Enter on your keyboard or click the green checkmark (✓).

✓ Result: The database is updated immediately, the UI confirms with "Saved [Product Name]", and visitors on p31market.com immediately see the new price.

3. Method 2: Full Product Edit & Sale/Compare-at Prices

When you want to put an item on sale or change name, category, stock status, or description alongside the price:

  1. In Curators & shops, locate the product and click the Edit button.
  2. Price ($): Enter the active selling price (what the customer will actually pay, e.g. $25.00).
  3. Compare-at price ($): Enter the original price (e.g. $35.00). When this field is higher than the selling price, the marketplace automatically displays a Sale tag with the original price struck through ($35.00 $25.00).
  4. Click Save.

4. Method 3: Creating a Brand New Product

Admins can upload and publish new products on behalf of any vendor or for the marketplace:

  1. Click the gold `+ Add product` button (found in the top right of the shop detail view, or in the All Products tab).
  2. Select Shop: If using the All Products tab, choose which shop/curator will own the product.
  3. Fill in Details:
    • Product Name: Title of the item (e.g., "Myrrh & Gold Botanical Oil").
    • Price ($): Required selling price.
    • Compare Price ($): Optional original price for discounted goods.
    • Category: Choose from presets (Collection, Skin, Wellness, Home, Art, Apparel, Jewelry, Gourmet) or type a custom category.
    • Availability: Select In stock, Sold out, Limited edition, or Pre-order.
    • Inventory: Optional remaining item quantity.
    • Photo: Click Upload photo from device to select any picture from your phone/computer (automatically uploaded to secure product storage), or paste an external URL.
    • Badge: Enter custom tags like "Bestseller", "Handmade", or "Staff Pick".
    • Description: Artisan notes, ingredients, dimensions, and craft details.
  4. Visibility: Leave "Active" checked so it displays in the shop immediately.
  5. Click Create product.

5. Method 4: Changing Storefront Subscription Terms

To modify what curators pay to host their storefronts on P31 Marketplace:

Setting Default Value Description / Impact
Monthly Price ($) $14.99 The monthly storefront hosting rate billed to vendors after their free period.
Free Months 3 Months The duration of the complimentary hosting period given upon vendor approval.
Reminder Days 14 Days Days before the free period expires when automated renewal reminders are sent.
Grace Days 7 Days Extra days permitted after expiration before a shop is paused.
Auto-Pause Switch Disabled (Off) When enabled, unpaid storefronts pause automatically when grace days pass.

To update terms: Go to Systems → Curators & shops → Storefront billing, click the Terms button, adjust the values, and click Save terms.

6. Administrator Best Practices & Checklist

  • Minimum Price for Card Checkout: Any product selling through Stripe card checkout must have a minimum price of $0.50.
  • Sale Badges: Always ensure compare_at_price is higher than price for the discount badge to calculate correctly.
  • Hiding Without Deleting: To temporarily take an item off the site without deleting its photos or description, tap the Eye (👁) icon to hide it. Tap it again anytime to unhide.
  • Featured Items: Click the Star (★) icon to pin high-priority products to the top of the artisan's boutique.
