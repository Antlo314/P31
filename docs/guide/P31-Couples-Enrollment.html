Couples enrollment: two signers, one payment

How to send a couple their enrollment, and what each partner sees. Live on the site since October 22, 2026.

How to send it

  1. In your mentor console, open Agreements and click Send the agreements.
  2. Choose Couples Private Mentorship. Two extra boxes appear for the partner’s email and name.
  3. Click Create and email both links. Each partner receives their own private link by email. You can also copy either link.

What happens next

  • Each partner signs all three forms on their own link: the Coaching Agreement, the Confidentiality Agreement and the Client Safety & Emergency Contact form. Each gets PDF copies, and every signed form goes to founder@thep31collective.org (members@ also receives the safety forms).
  • Payment stays locked until BOTH partners have signed all three forms. Whoever finishes first sees “Waiting for [partner]”, with their partner’s progress.
  • Once both have signed, either partner chooses the plan and pays once for both. The other partner is told payment is being handled, so nobody can pay twice.
  • When the payment goes through, both partners are enrolled. The partner who paid gets the usual welcome email. The other partner gets their own welcome email asking them to sign in, or create a login, with their email address. Their classroom opens automatically.
  • Sessions: both partners book on the 90-minute calendar.

At a glance: in your Agreements list, each partner’s card shows “Couple with [name]” and how many forms the other partner has signed, so you can see who you are waiting on.

Good to know

  • Each partner needs their own email address.
  • The partner’s classroom access follows the paid membership: if it renews, falls behind, is canceled or ends, the partner’s access does the same.
  • The partner’s Billing page explains that their partner’s payment covers them.
