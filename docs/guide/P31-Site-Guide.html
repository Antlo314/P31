Section 5

The vendor's Curator Studio

Where each vendor builds and runs her shop, on a phone or a computer. A guided tour opens the first time she signs in and replays from Help & Guide.

How a vendor opens a store, start to finish

  1. Apply at p31market.com/apply. The team approves it in Systems → Vendor applications (this pre-approves her email).
  2. Sign the Storefront Agreement at p31market.com/vendors/agreement. It attaches to her account automatically when the emails match.
  3. Create the account at p31market.com/register with the same email. Her studio opens.
  4. Identity: business name, tagline, story (Write with AI, once AI writing is on), shop link, city, Instagram, public email. Save.
  5. Storefront: banner, logo, and how she gets paid (Cash App, Venmo or a payment link today; Connect with Stripe once card checkout is on).
  6. Products: Add Artifact, start from a template, photo (Photo Studio cleans the background), price, options, badge, sale timer. Up to ten.
  7. Store Design: one of six templates, colour, fonts, layout. Publish design.
  8. Go live: if the team approved her email, the shop is live at her link immediately.

Storefront billing (from the Storefront Agreement)

  • Complimentary for the first three months from activation, then $14.99 a month if the vendor keeps the storefront; the 5% fee per sale applies throughout.
  • A shop approved from now on starts its free period by itself. Shops that were approved earlier are not billed until an admin presses "Start for N approved shops" in Systems → Curators & shops. Early-bird shops stay complimentary.
  • The vendor gets an email 14 days before the free period ends and a note when it ends. Pausing an unpaid shop automatically is off unless an admin switches it on under Terms.
  • Card payment for the subscription needs Stripe (section 11, item 1). Until then the button explains that card payments are not connected and points the vendor to vendor@p31market.com.
