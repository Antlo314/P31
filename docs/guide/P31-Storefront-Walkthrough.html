9

The team says yes

Systems → Curators & shops: open her shop, look it over, press Approved (or Needs changes with a note). From today she is emailed “Your shop is live” at that moment. Feature her in the directory, give badges, extend her free period, or pause the shop from the same panel.

The shopper’s path

From the directory to paying her

Every shop is reachable three ways: the Curators & shops directory, the Shop marketplace, and her own link.

The shopper’s path · on a phone

Choose, bag, request, pay

Most shoppers will do this on a phone. One bag holds items from several shops; each shop gets its own request.

What happens behind the request

  • The order is saved in the vendor’s studio under Orders, and in Systems → Orders for the team.
  • The shopper is emailed “Your order request with … (#P31-…)” with how to pay. The vendor is emailed the order with the shopper’s details.
  • The vendor confirms, marks it paid and fulfilled, and the shopper is emailed at each step.
  • Once Stripe is on, shops that connect it also get Pay by card in the bag, and the request stays as the other way to pay.

Your turn

Switch on today’s fixes, then decide

The same three steps as always, then a few calls that are yours.

  1. Run the database update (v48) from the project folder:
    npx supabase db query --linked --project-ref xsnhxjttdizljaawpumz -f storefront_v48_shop_emails.sql
    It answers with one row: signup_column true, live_column true.
  2. Deploy the two functions that send the shop emails and run the watchdog:
    npx supabase functions deploy join-notify watchdog --no-verify-jwt --use-api --project-ref xsnhxjttdizljaawpumz
  3. Put the site live: tell Claude “merge it”. This also takes the coaching forms and the content autopilot live, which are waiting on the same branch.
