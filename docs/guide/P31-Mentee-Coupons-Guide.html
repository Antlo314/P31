1. Executive Summary

Promotion codes are now fully integrated and live on your Stripe checkout flow for mentee enrollment. When prospective mentees receive their private invitation link (/enroll/:token) after their intro consultation, they will now see an "Add promotion code" option directly on Stripe's secure checkout page.

Stripe automatically verifies each coupon in real-time, calculates the discount, applies the duration limitation (e.g., 3 months, 6 months, or ongoing), and charges the adjusted amount. When the promotional window ends, Stripe will automatically bill at the standard recurring program rate without requiring any manual adjustments.

3. How It Works (The Complete Mentee Journey)

Step 1 · Invitation & Forms

Mentor sends the applicant their unique invitation link (/enroll/:token). The mentee signs the three required legal agreements (Business Coaching, Confidentiality, Emergency Safety).

Step 2 · Checkout with Code

Mentee clicks "Continue to secure checkout". On Stripe's checkout page, they click "Add promotion code", type their assigned code (e.g. STUDENT10), and click Apply.

Step 3 · Instant Discount & Card Processing

Stripe deducts the discount in real time. If 100% off (GRANTU100), no charge is drafted; Stripe securely registers the card on file for future renewals after 3 months.

Step 4 · Classroom Opens Automatically

The webhook triggers immediately upon completion: mentee status becomes active, their Collective Academy classroom unlocks, and they receive their welcome email with Calendly session links.

4. How the Owner / Team Can Test It

  1. Inspect in Stripe Dashboard: Log in to your Stripe Dashboard (dashboard.stripe.com) → Go to Product Catalog → Coupons. You will see all eight coupons active with their exact percentages and expiration schedules.
  2. Check Promotion Codes: Under the Promotion codes tab, verify that each customer-facing code (e.g., GRANTU100, STUDENT10, GRANTU50) is listed as Active.
  3. Test Live on an Enrollment Link: Open any mentee invitation URL (or generate a test invitation from Systems → Mentorship). Click "Continue to secure checkout". On the Stripe screen:
    • Click "Add promotion code" on the left summary column.
    • Enter STUDENT10 or GRANTU20 and click Apply.
    • Watch the price adjust downward automatically before submitting!
  4. Reviewing Orders: Any enrollment completed with a promo code will show the original price, the coupon code used, and the discounted total in Systems → Orders and your Stripe Payments ledger.
