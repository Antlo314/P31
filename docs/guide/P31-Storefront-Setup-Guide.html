How to set up and run your P31 storefront

Six steps from a new account to a shop that takes orders, shown on a phone and on a computer. Take them in any order; your studio keeps track.

WhereSign in at p31market.com/login. Your studio opens on the Identity page with your setup list.
What it costsFree for 3 months, then $14.99 a month, plus 5% of each sale. Up to 10 products.
How you are paidShoppers pay by card or Apple Pay through Stripe. The money goes to your bank.
HelpEmail vendor@p31market.com, or write to the team from Concierge in your studio.

Before you start

Your studio and your setup list

The setup list at the top of your studio shows what is done and what is next. Each line has a button that takes you to the right place.

  1. Shop name and shop link: what shoppers see, and your web address.
  2. Logo and banner photo: your look at the top of the shop.
  3. About your shop: a few lines about you and what you make.
  4. Your first product: a photo, a name and a price.
  5. Get paid: connect Stripe: so shoppers can pay you.
  6. Read and sign the Storefront Agreement.

A short tour opens the first time you sign in. It walks through each part of the studio in about two minutes. See it again any time with Show me around under your setup list.

Nothing you type is lost. If your phone reloads the page, you switch apps, or the connection drops, what you typed is kept on that device until you press Save.

Step 1 and step 3

Your shop name, link and story

Identity page, under Your shop details.

  1. Type your shop name. Your shop link fills in from it (for example p31market.com/luxe-candles). You can change it: small letters, numbers and dashes.
  2. Add a one-line tagline and a few lines about your shop. The AI button can write a first draft from your notes.
  3. Add your city, Instagram and the email shoppers can write to (all optional).
  4. Press Save shop details. Fields marked * are the only ones you need.

“That shop link is already taken” means another shop uses it. Add your city or a word, for example luxe-candles-atl, and save again.

Step 2 and step 4

Your logo, banner and products

Storefront page. Phone photos are perfect; they are resized for you.

  1. Tap Add product. Pick a template if one fits (it fills in a starting description and options).
  2. Tap the circle to add photos, up to 10. The first one is the main photo.
  3. Fill in the product name and price (needed), then anything else you like.
  4. Press Save product. To take a product down for a while, edit it and untick Visible in my storefront.

iPhone photo will not upload? Some iPhone photos are saved in a format (HEIC) that some browsers cannot open. Take a screenshot of the photo and upload the screenshot, or set iPhone Settings > Camera > Formats to Most Compatible.

Step 5

Get paid: connect Stripe

Storefront page, under Card payments (Stripe). About five minutes; have your bank details and ID handy.

  1. Tap Connect with Stripe. You go to Stripe’s own site; sign in or create a free account there.
  2. Fill in your details and your bank account. Stripe brings you back to your studio when you finish.
  3. If it says Not finished, tap Finish Stripe setup and complete what Stripe asks for, then Refresh status.

Shoppers then pay by card, Apple Pay and more right on your shop. Stripe sends your money to your bank on its usual payout schedule; Open my Stripe dashboard shows every payment and payout.

Step 6, and going live

The agreement, approval and your live shop

The last line of your setup list opens the Storefront Agreement: the fees, the benefits and your part, word for word. Sign it with your finger on a phone.

Good habits

  • Check Orders every day while your shop is new.
  • Use Marketing for a product link, a QR code and a promo picture to share.
  • Discounts makes codes like WELCOME10 for checkout.
  • Keep your products up to date: hide sold-out items or mark them Sold out.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does it cost?

Your storefront is free for the first 3 months from approval. After that it is $14.99 a month, and P31 keeps 5% of each sale. We email you before anything is charged, and you can cancel with two weeks’ notice.

How many products can I list?

Up to 10. To swap one out, hide or delete an old one. More product slots can be added under Add-ons & Featured.

How and when do I get paid?

Through Stripe. Shoppers pay by card or Apple Pay on your shop; P31 keeps its 5%, and the rest goes to your bank account on Stripe’s payout schedule. You can see every payment in your Stripe dashboard.

What does “Waiting for P31 to approve” mean?

The P31 team reviews every new shop. While you wait you can finish your setup; your shop stays private (only you see it) until it is approved, and you get an email when it goes live.

I typed my details and they disappeared.

Your studio now keeps what you type on your device until you press Save, and warns you before you leave with unsaved changes. If something still goes missing, sign out and back in, then email us what happened.

My photo will not upload.

Try a screenshot of the photo, or a different photo. Very large photos are made smaller for you. If it still fails, email the photo to vendor@p31market.com and we will add it.

Who do I contact?

Email vendor@p31market.com, or open Concierge in your studio menu to write to the P31 team.
