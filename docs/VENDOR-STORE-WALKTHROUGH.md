# Opening a store on Proverbs 31 Marketplace

Start to finish, in plain steps. Part A is what the P31 team does; Part B is what the vendor does on her phone or computer. Most vendors finish Part B in under an hour.

---

## Part A — The team (before the vendor touches anything)

1. **She applies.** She fills in the vendor form at **p31market.com/apply** (or the team enters her from the Google Form). It lands in Systems → Market → **Vendor applications**.
2. **Say yes.** Open her application, set it to **Approved**, press **Email them**. She gets the welcome email from vendor@p31market.com with the link to create her account.
3. **Unlock her email address.** Sign in at p31market.com/login as an admin → Curator Studio → **Vendor Approvals** → add her first name, last name and **the exact email she will sign up with**. This is the step that matters: when that email registers, her studio opens automatically (approved, verified, published). Without it she sees "Establishment Pending" after signing up.

That is all the team does until her shop is built.

---

## Part B — The vendor

### 1. Create the account (2 minutes)
1. Go to **p31market.com/register**.
2. Enter your name, your **business name**, the **same email the team approved**, and a password you choose.
3. Press Create account. You land in the **Curator Studio**; a short guided tour starts. Tap through it or close it, you can reopen it any time from **Help & Guide**.

### 2. Identity — who you are (10 minutes)
In the **Identity** tab:
1. **Business name** and a one-line **tagline** ("Small-batch candles, poured in Decatur").
2. **Your story** (the About section on your shop). Two to four short paragraphs: who you are, what you make, why. Press **Write with AI** for a draft if you are stuck, then make it yours.
3. **Shop link** (the vanity URL). Lowercase, no spaces: `p31market.com/your-shop-name`. This is the link you will share everywhere, so pick it once.
4. **Location** (city, state), **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **website**, **phone**, **public email** (the one shoppers can write to).
5. Press **Save**.

### 3. Storefront — how you look and how you get paid (10 minutes)
In the **Storefront** tab, top box **Shop Aesthetics & Payments**:
1. **Banner** — the wide picture across the top. A photo of your products, your table at a market, or your workspace. Landscape photos work best.
2. **Logo** — a square image. Your logo, or your favorite product shot.
3. **How you get paid.** Pick one or more:
   - **Connect with Stripe** (recommended): press the button, follow Stripe's steps (no codes to copy). Shoppers then pay by card right on your page and the money goes to your bank. Orders show in your **Orders** tab.
   - **Cash App** tag, **Venmo** handle, or a **payment link** as backups.
4. Press **Save Settings**.

### 4. Products (15 minutes for the first few)
Still in **Storefront**, press the gold **Add Artifact** button:
1. **Start from a template** (optional): Candle, Jewelry, Skincare, Apparel, Art print, Baked goods, Wellness, Home goods, Digital/service. It fills the category, the options shoppers pick (Scent, Size…), tags and a description you edit.
2. **Photo.** Tap the circle to upload. Tip: natural daylight near a window, plain background, product in the middle. Press **Remove background & stage in Photo Studio** to put it on a clean backdrop in one tap. Add more photos to the gallery (up to 10).
3. **Name**, **price**, **category**, **availability** (In stock / Sold out / Limited / Pre-order).
4. **Description**: what it is, what it is made of, why people love it. **Write description with AI** looks at your photo and drafts it.
5. Optional: **compare-at price** (shows a Sale tag), **inventory** (shows "Only 3 left"), **SKU**, **tags**, **options** (Size: S, M, L).
6. **Marketing box**: a **badge** on the photo (New, Bestseller, Limited, Handmade, Gift, Back in stock, Small batch, Market exclusive), **Sale ends** (shoppers see a countdown; the sale tag drops by itself when it passes), **Feature it first in my shop**.
7. **Save Artifact.** Repeat for each product (up to 10). The gear icon on a card edits it; untick "Visible in my storefront" to hide one without deleting it; "Feature it first" pins it to the top.

### 5. Store Design — pick your look (3 minutes)
In **Store Design**:
1. Choose a **template**: Atelier (the P31 look), Boutique (jewelry & beauty), Gallery (art & prints), Editorial (magazine spreads), Market (food & wellness), Noir Luxe (evening wear & fragrance).
2. Tune the **accent colour**, **headings** (serif or sans), **hero** style, **products per row**, and whether products or your story come first.
3. The preview updates as you go. Press **Publish design**, then **View my storefront** to see exactly what shoppers see.

### 6. Discounts and testimonials (optional, 5 minutes)
- **Discounts**: create a code (WELCOME10, 10% off, ends Sunday). Shoppers enter it at checkout.
- **Testimonials**: paste what customers have said about you; they show on your shop.

### 7. Marketing — get seen (as long as you like)
In the **Marketing** tab:
- **Promote a product**: every product has its own link and QR code, a ready-made **promo image** in four looks and three sizes (post, portrait, story), and one tap that writes captions, hook lines, hashtags, an email and a text message about it.
- **Followers & email**: shoppers leave their email at the bottom of your shop. Write to them (AI drafts it), send yourself a test, then send to everyone. One send a day.
- **Share kit**: your shop link, a bio line for Instagram, ready-to-post lines, and a printable **table sign** with your QR for the market.
- **Insights**: which products people open, buy and share.

### 8. Go live
- If the team unlocked your email (Part A, step 3), **your shop is already live** at your link, listed under Curators and in the Shop.
- If not, press **Submit for Review** on the Identity tab. The team approves it in Vendor Approvals and you get a note in **Concierge**.

### Every week after that
1. Share one product link or promo image (Marketing → Promote a product).
2. Keep prices, stock and badges honest (Storefront).
3. Check **Orders** and reply to buyers; check **Concierge** for notes from the team.
4. Before a market: email your followers the date and your booth, and print the table sign.
