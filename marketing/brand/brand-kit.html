01 · The mark

Two hands, one circle

The hands are the whole story: one lifting, one receiving, inside a ring that is never closed. Plum for the hand that gives, gold for the hand that is raised. It is the single brand mark for everything P31 does.

Full color
On white, ivory and photos

Full color on night
Dark sections, video end cards

White
On plum, gold or busy photos

Plum
Single-color print, stamps, embroidery

Clear space and size

keep a space the width of the gold hand's thumb on every sidesmallest: 24 px on screen · 0.4 in in print

App icon and favicon

The mark sits on a plum-to-night tile for icons and profile pictures, so it reads at 32 px. Use the round version for Instagram, Facebook, Google and email avatars.

Do use the mark exactly as supplied · give it room · put it on plum, night, ivory, white or a calm photo · use white on busy backgrounds.
Don't stretch, rotate or recolor it · add drop shadows or outlines · place it over text · redraw the hands · use the old emblems next to it.

02 · Color

Plum, gold and ivory

Plum is the lead. Gold is the accent, used sparingly, where the eye should land. Ivory is the ground on the marketplace; night is the ground on the Collective and in video.

Pairings that always work

  • Ivory page · ink text · plum headings · gold for one accent per screen (a button, a line, an italic word).
  • Night section · white text · gold-light accent · plum only as a soft glow or gradient.
  • Gold buttons carry dark text (#2A1A00). Plum buttons carry white. Never gold text on plum below 18 pt.

03 · Type

Noto Serif and Manrope

Where her gifts make room.

Noto Serif for headlines and pull quotes. Regular weight, tight line height, one italic word in gold for emphasis. Never bold headlines; the italic does the work.

Eyebrow label Body copy in Manrope, 15–17 px on screen, comfortable line height.

Manrope for everything else: body, buttons (800 weight), labels (uppercase, letter-spaced), numbers. Buttons are pills with no borders.

04 · Voice

Warm, elegant, confident

How we sound

  • Faith-rooted without preaching. All are welcome.
  • Celebrates the woman and her craft. Specific over clever.
  • Short sentences. Plain words. One idea at a time.
  • Scripture with care: KJV, quoted exactly, one verse at most. Anchor: Proverbs 31:31.
  • We never invent prices, dates or claims. If it's not confirmed, we don't say it.

Lines we return to

“Where her gifts make room.”

“Heal. Rise. Build. Prosper God’s way.”

“Give her of the fruit of her hands; and let her own works praise her in the gates.” — Proverbs 31:31

05 · Social and video

Reels that hold

The first 2 seconds

A hook on screen in big words: a promise, a question or a number. No intros, no logos first. Cut or change the shot every 1.5–3 seconds.

Captions on, safe zone

Captions always on, inside the safe zone (clear of the bottom 22% and right 10% where Instagram draws its own buttons). Bold caps or karaoke styles for energy, serif italic for story.

Caption copy

Hook first, keywords people search, "link in bio" on Instagram, 3–5 specific hashtags. Post when the audience is active (Studio shows the best times), then reply fast.
