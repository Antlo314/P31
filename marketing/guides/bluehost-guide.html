P31 Marketplace & Collective

Bluehost guide · domains & team email

Bluehost is where both P31 web addresses, p31market.com and thep31collective.org, are registered and managed. It doesn’t run the websites themselves (they run on Vercel), but it holds the “address book” that tells the internet where everything lives: the websites, every team inbox, and the proof that our emails are really from us. If a setting here is wrong, a site can stop loading, emails can bounce, and messages can land in spam.

Bluehost guide Team email

3 Sign in to Bluehost

  1. Go to bluehost.com and click Login.
  2. Sign in with the account that owns both domains. You’ll land in the Bluehost Portal.
Turn on two-step login for this account (in your Bluehost profile or security settings). Whoever controls this login controls both websites and every team inbox.

4 Check the team inboxes

Open the list of email addresses:

  1. In the left menu, click Hosting, then My Plans.
  2. On the right, click cPanel Email.
  3. In the pop-up, click Email Accounts, then Continue.

5 Add any missing address

Option A: a full inbox (the person logs in to read it)

  1. In Email Accounts, click + Create (top right).
  2. Pick the Domain, type the Username (for example vendor), and set a strong Password.
  3. Click Edit Settings and raise the storage. Starter plans allow only 100 MB, and a full inbox bounces new mail.
  4. Click + Create.

Option B: a forwarder (mail goes to an inbox they already use)

  1. Go back to cPanel Email and choose Forwarders.
  2. Click Add Forwarder. Type the address (for example grants), choose the domain, then enter the inbox it should go to.
  3. Click Add Forwarder.
Bluehost turns off “catch-all” addresses on shared plans, so every address the website uses must exist as an inbox or a forwarder. Mail to an address that doesn’t exist simply bounces.

6 Test every inbox (5 minutes)

  1. From a personal email (Gmail, iCloud…), send one message with the subject “P31 inbox test” to all ten addresses above.
  2. A reply saying “Address not found” or “Undeliverable” means that address is missing. Go back to step 5.
  3. Ask each person to reply “got it” from their P31 address. That proves the inbox can both receive and send.
  4. If a reply lands in your spam folder, mark it Not spam and let us know.
Bluehost guide Domain settings (DNS)

7 Find the domain settings (DNS)

  1. In the Bluehost Portal’s left menu, click Domains.
  2. Click the domain (p31market.com or thep31collective.org).
  3. Open the DNS tab. This is the list of records described below.

The same records also appear in cPanel under Domains → Zone Editor. Use either one; they’re the same settings.

9 Adding a record when asked

Two services will each ask for a record. They change nothing else:

  • Google Search Console gives a TXT record starting with google-site-verification=. It proves we own the domain so Google lists our new pages.
  • Our email-sending service (for alerts, receipts and campaigns) gives a few records on its own sub-name, such as send or resend._domainkey.
  1. On the DNS tab, click + Add record.
  2. Choose the Type (TXT, MX or CNAME) exactly as the service shows it.
  3. Copy the Host / Name and the Value exactly. Use @ when the service says “root” or leaves the name blank.
  4. Click Add. Changes usually work within minutes, but can take up to a few hours.
Only one SPF record per domain. If a service asks you to add a record starting with v=spf1 on @, don’t create a second one. Send it to P31’s developer to merge into the existing record. Two SPF records make email fail.

10 Keep it safe

  • Auto-renew on: in Domains, confirm both domains are set to renew automatically and the card on file is current.
  • Two-step login on the Bluehost account (step 3).
  • Share access, not passwords: give the developer or team their own access instead of the main login.
  • Help is 24/7: Bluehost chat (bubble at the bottom right of bluehost.com) or phone 888-401-4678 (7am–midnight ET).
