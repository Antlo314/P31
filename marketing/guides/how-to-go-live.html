How to go live · mentor guide

Start a live video class right inside the Collective — no Zoom or Google app needed. Students join from their phone or computer, and every call is saved: who came, when, for how long, attendance, and recordings.

0 One time only: turn on call tracking

  1. Go to www.thep31collective.org/systems and sign in.
  2. Tap Academy in the menu.
  3. Under Live classes, tap Turn on call tracking.
  4. You’ll see a green message: “Call tracking is on.” That’s it — you never need to do this again.

1 Go live now

  1. Go to www.thep31collective.org/portal and sign in.
  2. Choose Business Mentorship or Faith-Based Mentorship (your mentor console).
  3. At the top of the page, tap Go live now.
  4. Fill in the box:
    • What’s it called? — e.g. “Monday Strategy Session.”
    • Who’s invited — Everyone, or Choose students and tick names.
    • About how long — 30 to 120 minutes.
    • Allow recording — tick it if you want to record (see costs on page 3).
  5. Tap Go live. The room opens right inside the site.
  6. When the browser asks, tap Allow for your camera and microphone.
  7. On the check-in screen, make sure you can see and hear yourself, then tap Join.
Your students are told right away. Everyone you invited gets a “Live now” notification. One tap takes them straight into the call.

2 Call one student

  1. In your console, tap Students and choose the student.
  2. Tap Call now, then Go live. Only that student is invited.

3 In the call

Use the bar at the bottom of the video:

  • Microphone / camera — turn yours on or off.
  • Share screen — show slides, a worksheet or a website.
  • Chat — type links and notes for everyone.
  • Record — appears for you when Allow recording was ticked.
  • Leave — you leave; the class keeps going for others.
To finish the class for everyone, tap End for everyone at the top of the page (above the video).

4 Schedule a live class for later

  1. In your console, tap Sessions, then Schedule a session.
  2. Add a title, the day and time, and the length.
  3. Under Where the class happens, keep Live room in the classroom selected.
  4. Tap Schedule. Students are notified and see it on their Sessions page.
  5. At class time, open Sessions and tap Start class.
Rooms open 30 minutes before the start time, so you and your students can arrive early.

5 After the call: your report

  1. Tap Sessions, find the class, and tap Report.
  2. You’ll see each person, when they joined and left, how many minutes they stayed, and their attendance.
  3. If you recorded, tap Show recordings. Students can rewatch from their Sessions page too.

Every call is also added to each person’s timeline in Systems → CRM.

6 How students join

From the notification
Tap the bell → “Live now: …” → the call opens.
From the classroom
Sessions → Join class now. Joining marks them present (or late after 10 minutes).

7 Good to know

Best browsersChrome, Safari or Edge, kept up to date. Phones and tablets work too.
Camera or mic blocked?Tap the lock icon by the web address → allow Camera and Microphone → reload the page.
“Live video isn’t connected yet”Ask your P31 admin — the Daily key needs to be added in Supabase.
Free minutes10,000 participant-minutes every month (e.g. 33 hours of a 5-person class). After that, $0.004 per person per minute — calls are never cut off.
RecordingAbout $0.81 per recorded hour (needs a card on the Daily account).
Looking your bestLight in front of you, camera at eye level, headphones to avoid echo.
